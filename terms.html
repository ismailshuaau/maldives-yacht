Marketplace terms

Maldives Liveaboard Booking connects guests with independent yacht operators. A reservation request creates a temporary availability hold; the applicable price, itinerary, inclusions, payment terms, and cancellation terms are shown during booking and confirmed by the operator.

Routes may change for weather, sea conditions, safety, or local guidance. Reviews may be submitted only for completed bookings and are moderated for authenticity and platform standards.

Do not share account credentials or booking access tokens. Misuse, fraud, or attempts to interfere with marketplace operation may lead to suspension.
