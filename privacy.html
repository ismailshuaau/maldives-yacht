Privacy

We use account, booking, payment-status, wishlist, review, and support information to provide the marketplace services you request. Payment card details are handled by the payment provider and are not collected by this platform.

Booking access tokens are credentials. Keep them private. They are used to attach an anonymous reservation to an account and are not replaced by email matching.

Contact support to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. Operational, financial, security, and audit records may be retained where required.
